**Meridian Heat** is a complete 2D top-down open-world crime game set in Port Meridian, an original sun-soaked coastal city of my own invention — nothing here is taken from any commercial game. Explore five districts on foot and by car, steal traffic cars, outrun the harbour police as your wanted level climbs, fight back with fists and a pistol, pick up cash and first-aid kits, and run three story missions for Vera Kline and Eddie Rook: a harbour pickup, a taxi war across Palm Heights, and a midnight run through the Industrial yards. A live HUD tracks cash, health, wanted stars and the clock, a minimap shows the whole city with police and mission markers, and a synthesized WebAudio soundtrack and sound effects play with no audio files. Progress — cash, score and missions — saves to localStorage, and the game runs entirely from static files in a desktop browser with no network requests and no libraries.

## Run it

Serve `game/` with any static server, then open `game/index.html` in desktop Chrome:

```sh
node serve.ts
```

Then open http://localhost:8080 and play with the keyboard and mouse: WASD to move and drive, E to enter or exit a car, mouse to aim, click to shoot, F to punch, M for missions, Space for the handbrake, P to pause.
